Give your enterprise customers control over what your AI agents can do in their account.

Reach is the drop-in policy and audit layer for B2B SaaS agents. Let each customer set their own action limits, route high-risk calls to their own approvers, and export a tamper-evident log of every run, while your engineers replay new rules against real traffic before they go live.

From per-customer rules to proof your buyers can trust.

  1. Step 1

    Pass your tenant and user context

    Wrap your tools or MCP servers with a lightweight proxy or SDK, passing the tenant and user IDs you already have from Clerk, Cognito, Auth0 or your own tokens.

  2. Step 2

    Enforce per-customer limits

    Apply workspace-specific caps, allowed actions and thresholds for each enterprise customer, without hardcoding if/else branches.

  3. Step 3

    Route approvals to the customer

    Pause high-risk actions and send the approval request straight to that customer's Slack channel, webhook or in-app queue.

  4. Step 4

    Replay rules and prove every action

    Dry-run policy changes against recorded past runs before they go live, and give every customer an exportable, tamper-evident log of what your agent did.

    action: require_approval
    approvers: "acme:#finance-approvals"

    action: block
    reason: "Acme Corp disabled autonomous cancellations"
